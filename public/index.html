 Clear boundaries
What this does.
And what it doesn’t.

      

        
Does this work in every browser today?
No. WebMCP support is emerging and may require a Chrome origin trial. Handoff detects support at runtime. Other visitors keep your ordinary form.

        
Will an agent be able to book or buy silently?
Not with this package. The included workflow only prepares the quote form. The customer must review and submit it.

        
Does Handoff send data to an AI provider?
No. The generated script contains your business name, services, validation, and form-mapping logic. It has no analytics, remote calls, or AI-provider integration.

        
What happens after I request a pilot?
You’ll receive a confirmation and the proposed scope will be reviewed against your existing form. Submitting the request does not charge you or authorize client-site changes. Scope and next steps come before any invoice.

        
Is this an AI-search ranking tool?
No. It exposes a quote workflow to compatible browser agents. It does not promise rankings, citations, recommendations, or more traffic.
